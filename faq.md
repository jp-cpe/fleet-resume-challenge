# Challenge FAQ

### What is the Fleet Resume Challenge?
The Fleet Resume Challenge is a hands-on, project-based way to build and prove real device management skill using Fleet. By deploying Fleet, enrolling hosts, and working through tasks like GitOps, policies, software, and MDM, you gain practical experience you can put on your resume and talk about in interviews.

### Who should attempt the challenge?
Anyone looking to break into IT device management, as well as current IT professionals who want to sharpen their skills with a modern, open-source platform. It's also a great fit if you're preparing for Client Platform Engineering, Security Engineering, or related roles.

> While anyone can complete this challenge, it helps to have a background in IT or some foundational IT knowledge.

### How long does the challenge take?
It depends on your experience and how much time you can dedicate. Plan for anywhere from a few weekends to a few weeks. Reading-based tasks go quickly, while deploying Fleet, enrolling hosts, and configuring MDM take more time.

### How much does it cost?
Fleet itself is free and open source, so you can complete most of the challenge at no cost. Your may have some hosting expenses depending on which path you take.

### Do I need to complete the tasks in order?
Mostly, yes. Later tasks build on earlier ones, so you must deploy Fleet and enroll hosts before you can work on policies, software, and the rest. Within those constraints, feel free to explore.

### Do I have to do all the tasks?
No, but the more you complete, the more hands-on experience you'll have to show. Even a partial run-through is valuable, and finishing everything makes for the strongest resume story.

### What hardware and software do I need?
You'll need a place to host the Fleet server (cloud or local) and hosts running at least 3 different operating systems (for example macOS, Windows, and Linux) to enroll.

### Can I use VMs instead of physical devices?
Yes. Virtual machines are a great way to enroll hosts across multiple operating systems without buying extra hardware. Note that some MDM features (especially Apple MDM) work best with real devices.

### What skills or background do I need going in?
Some foundational IT knowledge helps: comfort with the command line, basic networking, and familiarity with Git and YAML (used for Fleet GitOps). You don't need to be an expert—the challenge is designed to build these skills.

### Is the Fleet GitOps certification required?
No, it's recommended but optional. Having the **Fleet Certified GitOps Administrator - Level 1** certification strengthens your resume, but you can complete the challenge without it.

### How do I get the GitOps certification?
The certification is obtained by attending a [Fleet GitOps workshop](https://fleetdm.com/gitops-workshop). Check the workshop page for the current schedule and details.

### What job does this prepare me for?
The challenge builds the skills used by Client Platform Engineers, Device Management / Endpoint engineers, and IT roles that manage fleets of devices.

### How do I show proof that I completed the challenge?
Keep your work in a public GitHub repo (GitOps configs, queries, policies), capture screenshots of your Fleet instance, and write the blog post from the final task describing what you learned.

### Will completing the challenge guarantee me a job?
No, but it gives you real, demonstrable device management experience to put on your resume and talk about in interviews, which sets you apart from candidates with only theoretical knowledge.

### Where can I get help if I'm stuck?
Start with the [Fleet documentation](https://fleetdm.com/docs) and join the [#fleet](https://macadmins.slack.com/archives/C0214NELAE7) channel of the Mac Admins Slack.

### How do I contribute to the challenge?
This challenge is a work in progress and contributions are welcome. Open an issue or pull request with improvements, fixes, or new ideas.
