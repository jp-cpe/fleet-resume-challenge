# 0. Get oriented

Before you stand up anything, understand what Fleet is and how it's put together.

1. Understand [why Fleet](https://fleetdm.com/docs/get-started/why-fleet) exists and the problems it solves.
2. Learn [Fleet's anatomy](https://fleetdm.com/docs/get-started/anatomy): the Fleet server, the `fleetd` agent, osquery, and how they fit together.
