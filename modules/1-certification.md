# 1. Certification

Your resume should ideally have the [Fleet Certified GitOps Administrator - Level 1](https://www.virtualbadge.io/certificate-validator?credential=e3c0b1ef-0560-43a9-8e14-db672128acd6) certification on it.

> **Note:** This certification is obtained by attending a [Fleet GitOps workshop](https://fleetdm.com/gitops-workshop).

### Is the Fleet GitOps certification required?
No, it's recommended but optional. Having the **Fleet Certified GitOps Administrator - Level 1** certification strengthens your resume, but you can complete the challenge without it.

### How do I get the GitOps certification?
The certification is obtained by attending a [Fleet GitOps workshop](https://fleetdm.com/gitops-workshop). Check the workshop page for the current schedule and details.
