# 15. Blog post

Write and publish a short blog post describing what you built and learned during the challenge.

## Goal
A published, publicly linkable blog post that tells the story of what you built in the Fleet Resume Challenge — the kind of post you can link from your resume and talk through in an interview.

## Resources
- [Putting Fleet on your resume](../guides/resume.md)
- [Cloud Resume Challenge](https://cloudresumechallenge.dev/) — inspiration for the write-up format
- A publishing platform: [dev.to](https://dev.to/), [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com/), [Medium](https://medium.com/), LinkedIn, or your own site

## Tasks
- [ ] Reflect on what you built and learned across the core modules
- [ ] Draft a post covering what Fleet is, what you deployed and configured, what was hard, and what surprised you
- [ ] Link your proof: your public GitOps repo, screenshots of your Fleet instance, and any other artifacts
- [ ] Publish it somewhere with a public URL

## Done when
- [ ] A blog post exists at a public URL you can link from your resume
